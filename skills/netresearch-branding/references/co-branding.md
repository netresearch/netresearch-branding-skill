## Purpose and activation

Netresearch presents topics from its business context visually: knowledge about platforms and methods, implementations, customer relationships. There the Netresearch design often meets a second brand, such as a platform (OroCommerce, TYPO3), a customer or a Netresearch product brand (Revyo Basecamp). An endorsement line connects both designs and names the role of Netresearch in a few words.

- **The default is Netresearch branding only.** Without an explicit request there is no endorsement line, no lockup and no colour of a second brand.
- **Activation needs an explicit request** from the user or a calling skill to combine Netresearch with a second brand: a platform, a Netresearch product brand or a customer.
- **A brand named as the topic is no such request.** A Netresearch landing page about OroCommerce features stays Netresearch branding only.
- **Out of scope:** output where another brand is the sender, such as platform-only material or material in a customer's corporate design. The Hard Validity Rules have no exception for it.

## Frame rule

Netresearch is the sender, and every Hard Validity Rule in `SKILL.md` applies unchanged: Netresearch logo in the header, Netresearch colours, typography and footer.

- The sender sets the frame: logo position, footer, structural colours and fonts.
- The second brand appears as a content accent, for example as chapter colour, highlight or accent bar. Its colours meet WCAG AA like any other colour pair.
- At most one other brand appears visually next to Netresearch (logo, colour accent). A line may name further brands as text, for example a customer and its platform.
- The endorsement line names the role of Netresearch.

## Terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Endorsement line | Short line in which one brand vouches for another or names its role, e.g. "explained by Netresearch" |
| Co-branding lockup | Fixed arrangement of both logos, a divider and the endorsement line |
| Claim | "enable & connect", the Netresearch mission. It stays unchanged next to the logo; endorsement lines may inflect it ("enabled & connected by Netresearch") |

## Roles of Netresearch

The examples illustrate, the list is open. For a new case, determine the role first, then pick a line from a matching group of the catalog.

| Role | Examples | Matching groups |

| Implementer | Netresearch implements OroCommerce or TYPO3 systems; Netresearch tailors OroCommerce for a customer | Building and implementing, Technical foundation, Audience |

### Examples by use case

The customer name "Example Retail" is fictitious. The chosen line must match the service actually delivered, and publishing a customer's name needs that customer's approval. The strongest variant comes first.

| Use case | Brands involved | Example lines |
|---|---|---|
| Explainer format on a platform | Netresearch + platform | "OroCommerce explained by Netresearch" · "The Netresearch Guide to TYPO3" · "TYPO3 Enablement by Netresearch" |
| Methods from Revyo Basecamp | Revyo Basecamp + Netresearch | "Revyo Basecamp powered by Netresearch" · "Basecamp Methods explained by Netresearch" · "Revyo Basecamp Enablement by Netresearch" |
| Platform implementation | Netresearch + platform | "OroCommerce, implemented by Netresearch" · "OroCommerce, tailored by Netresearch" · "TYPO3 website built by Netresearch" |
| Platform for a customer | customer + platform | "OroCommerce tailored for Example Retail" · "Example Retail on OroCommerce" · "OroCommerce at Example Retail" (case study) · "OroCommerce made for Example Retail" |
| Platform for a customer, with Netresearch | customer + platform + Netresearch | "Example Retail on OroCommerce · implemented by Netresearch" · "OroCommerce, tailored by Netresearch for Example Retail" |
| Integration | platform + system + Netresearch | "OroCommerce and [ERP], connected by Netresearch" · "Connecting OroCommerce and [ERP], explained by Netresearch" |
| Customer relationship | customer + Netresearch | "Example Retail in partnership with Netresearch" · "Example Retail, enabled by Netresearch" · "Example Retail Shop, managed by Netresearch" |
| Information for a customer | customer + Netresearch | "Prepared for Example Retail by Netresearch" |
| References | customer logos + Netresearch | "Trusted by" above the logo row |

A reference wall is a row of customer logos under "Trusted by", not a co-branding lockup; the one-brand limit of the frame rule does not apply to it, the logo approval does.

## Selection procedure

1. Check the request. Without an explicit co-branding request, stay with Netresearch branding only.
2. Determine the role of Netresearch from the roles table.
3. Pick a matching group, then a line. Read its *Recommendation*.
4. Check the line against the service actually delivered and against the guardrails.
5. Build the output in the Netresearch frame and use the second brand only as a content accent.

## Guardrails

- **Logos:** never altered, recoloured, distorted, filtered or rebuilt, for any brand.
- **Platform logos** (e.g. OroCommerce, TYPO3): only as the vendor's or partner programme's brand guidelines allow. Check them before use.
- **Partner badges:** only with a documented partner status and as the vendor prescribes.
- **Customer logos and names:** in published material only with the customer's approval.
- **Contrast:** WCAG AA for every colour pair, including the second brand's colours (`scripts/contrast-audit.cjs`).
- **Platform brand first:** lines such as "OroCommerce tailored for …" put the other brand at the centre and extend its product name. Check them against the vendor's brand guidelines before publishing. With the customer brand first ("Example Retail on OroCommerce") the risk is lower.
- **Product lockups:** product-specific lockups, such as the one for Revyo Basecamp, live in that product's brand profile.

## Writing notes

- **Spelling:** "TYPO3" in capitals without a space, "OroCommerce" as one word.
- **Language:** one language per format series. German equivalents include "erklärt von", "umgesetzt von", "erstellt für".
- **Rejected:** presented for, made understandable by, conveyed by, educated by, based on, brought to you by. They are unidiomatic, misleading or say nothing about the role.
